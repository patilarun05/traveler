My rider story

I ride for the feeling that begins before sunrise, when the roads are still quiet and the machine settles into its own rhythm. That first stretch of highway always feels like a promise.

My Bullet has carried me through temple towns, hills, coastlines, and wind-beaten roads where the scenery keeps changing but the joy of riding stays exactly the same.

Why the Bullet matters

A Royal Enfield Bullet makes the journey feel honest. It is not only about reaching the destination quickly. It is about hearing the engine note, feeling the road texture, and respecting every kilometer you cover.

That is why these rides stay with me. They are not just trips across India. They are earned memories built mile by mile.

Places that stayed with me

Each stop has a different mood: spiritual calm, coastal openness, mountain freshness, or the pride that comes from reaching a place on your own bike.

Madurai

Madurai felt intense, historic, and alive. Riding into the city on my bike made the whole arrival feel grand, as if the road itself was leading into something timeless.

Heritage • energy • deep culture

Rameshwaram

Rameshwaram carried a calmer emotion. The road, the atmosphere, and the spiritual feeling of the place made the ride memorable long before the destination was fully in sight.

Devotion • calm • purpose

Dhanushkodi

Dhanushkodi felt raw and unforgettable. It was the kind of place where land, wind, and sea create the feeling that you have reached the far edge of the map.

Edge ride • wild beauty

Kanyakumari

Kanyakumari felt like a rider milestone. Reaching the southern tip on a Bullet brings pride, emotion, and the satisfaction of seeing a dream become real.

Milestone • southern tip • pride

Munnar

Munnar brought cool air, bends, and green landscapes that made every kilometer feel refreshing. It is the kind of ride that teaches you to slow down and absorb everything.

Hills • mist • calm thrill

West Coast Route

The west coast ride from Janjira Fort toward Kanyakumari felt cinematic. Changing coastlines, highway stretches, fishing towns, and sea breeze turned the entire route into a living memory.

Sea breeze • endurance • freedom
